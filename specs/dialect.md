# dialect

the convention layer of [[neural]]: an agreed-upon collection of [[sentence]]s and [[motif]]s. sentences and motifs are parole — individual utterances; a dialect is langue, the shared system. a dialect is what crystallizes when many [[neurons]] use the same forms the same way.

## what a dialect is

a dialect names a convention for linking — "to say X causes Y, make this shape." a [[neuron]] supplies intent; the dialect supplies structure, so an invocation produces a well-formed subgraph. a dialect is a [[smart contract]] over [[link]]s: it builds the right ones.

equivalently, at the frontier: a dialect is a low-motif-conductance community of the [[cybergraph]]; a graphon whose identity is its [[motif]]-density spectrum (so a φ\* filter trained on a small instance transfers to a larger one); and a sheaf of local meanings whose global consistency is a cohomology check. it is the attractor set of motifs that survives re-transmission — and a schism is a bifurcation of φ\*. see [[frontier]].

## bootloader and emergent

- bootloader — installed at genesis: TRUE and FALSE, the epistemic poles all meaning derives from
- emergent — discovered through convergent use: is-a, follows, causes, contradicts, part-of, see-also

dialects are found, not designed. when a form recurs and holds [[focus]], the [[tri-kernel]] in [[tru]] surfaces it as a stable structural position, and it enters the registry.

## hierarchy

dialects layer by [[topology]]:

```
structural → domain-specific
epistemic  → modal
temporal   → causal
social     → evaluative
```

## registry

active dialects are themselves [[particles]], ranked by [[focus]] like everything else — a dialect that loses adoption falls out of use. the high-focus dialects are the grammar the [[egregore]] currently speaks; the high-focus [[particles]] are its [[lexicon]].
